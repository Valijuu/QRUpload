# Changelog

All notable user-visible changes to this project. Format based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/).

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Pinch to zoom (up to 3x) and pan photos in the lightbox; double-tap zooms in and out.
- Full-screen mode in the lightbox that hides the toolbar and arrows, with real full screen where the browser supports it.
- Thank-you page with the wedding photo, a thank-you text and one button to the photos.
- Password login for the photo gallery, with a button to show or hide the typed password.
- Photo gallery with square tiles (2 columns on phones up to 6 on desktop), a lightbox with swipe and keyboard navigation, and download of single originals.
- Download of all photos as ZIP packages in original quality and in phone quality.

### Removed
- Invitation pages (schedule, food & drinks, wedding pros), the QR code token login and the Nextcloud upload/gallery links.
